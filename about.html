About us

A bearing company that owns its steel

From a single trading operation in 2004 to two manufacturing plants and a four-country sales network. Forging, heat treatment and final grinding happen under our own roof.

Timeline

2004
BSL Bearings founded

The company starts trading bearings and builds its first distributor relationships across Central Asia, Africa and the Middle East.

Dubai, UAE
Global sales HQ

Global sales are consolidated in Jebel Ali Freezone, Dubai — the shipping and coordination centre for every market we serve.

Bhiwandi, India
Sonale Bearing Engineering Co

Manufacturing plant producing ball and roller bearings, with forging and heat treatment carried out on site.

Liaocheng, China
Shandong BSL Precision Bearing Co

Second plant in Shandong province, adding capacity across the deep groove ball bearing series range.

Yakima, USA
BSL Bearings LLC

Sales office in Yakima, Washington, serving North American industrial and agricultural customers.
