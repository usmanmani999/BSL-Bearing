Who we are

Two plants,
four locations,
one standard

Founded in 2004, BSL Bearings has grown into a leading bearing brand across Central Asia, Africa, the Middle East and the USA. Global sales run from Jebel Ali Freezone in Dubai, supported by a sales office in Yakima, Washington.

Production sits with Sonale Bearing Engineering Co in Bhiwandi, India, and Shandong BSL Precision Bearing Co in Liaocheng, China. Roller bearings are forged and heat-treated in-house, so tolerance and hardness are controlled from raw steel through final inspection.
