Wheel hub bearings

A wheel hub bearing guides wheel rotation and carries both axial and radial load, so it is a critical component. A traditional wheel bearing is built from two sets of tapered roller bearings, or a combination of ball bearings, with installation, lubrication, sealing and clearance adjustment all carried out on the vehicle production line — difficult to assemble, costly, and demanding in service. The DAC (double angular contact) hub unit replaces that arrangement with a single sealed, pre-greased unit: clearance adjustment is omitted, weight and package size drop, load capacity is high, and the bearing needs no maintenance in service.

Taper roller bearings

Taper roller bearings take combined radial and axial load, and the steeper-angle types carry predominantly axial load. They are separable: the inner ring, with its roller and cage assembly, mounts independently of the outer ring.
